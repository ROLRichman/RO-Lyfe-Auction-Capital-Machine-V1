# County Research Playbook

Build a reusable county profile rather than relying on a single auction listing.

## Research order

1. County tax claim / tax sale office
2. County treasurer / tax collector
3. County clerk / recorder / court
4. County GIS / assessor / parcel search
5. State statutes / administrative sources
6. Auction platform
7. Commercial databases
8. General articles

## Profile questions

- What exactly is sold?
- How is it sold?
- What is the deposit?
- What is the payment deadline?
- What can redeem?
- What survives the sale?
- What is the title / deed process?
- What happens to unsold inventory?
- Is there an OTC or resale process?
- What is the county's exact terminology?

## Status labels

- RESEARCH REQUIRED
- PRIMARY SOURCE FOUND
- COUNTY CONFIRMED
- SECONDARY SOURCE ONLY
- CONFLICTING SOURCES
- NEEDS RE-VERIFICATION

## Rule

The exact current county terms and official documents control.
